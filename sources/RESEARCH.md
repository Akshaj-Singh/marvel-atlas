## Editorial safeguards

- Do not invent shared continuity when the evidence is only a similar appearance, actor, costume, title, or thematic parallel.
- An unreleased-film credit appearing in Fandom is **not** automatically elevated to official announcement. Check source date and studio confirmation.
- Surprise cameos and plot twists use spoiler level 3 where possible.
- A meaningful relationship should have a concrete explanation and a short path, not merely connect to an arbitrary “Marvel” hub.
- Franchise relationships and crossover bridges are not by themselves a claim of identical Earth designation.
