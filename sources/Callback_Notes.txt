The Evolution of Tony Stark (Iron Man)
1. "I am Iron Man."
	• Iron Man: Tony defiantly discards his prepared notes at a press conference to confess his identity to the world.
	• Avengers: Endgame: Tony echoes his own history right before snapping away Thanos's army, turning a boastful statement into a selfless final sacrifice.
2. "You're not the guy to make the sacrifice play, to lay down on a wire..."
	• The Avengers: Steve Rogers scolds Tony, claiming he only fights for himself.
	• Avengers: Endgame: Tony proves Steve wrong by laying down his life on the ultimate cosmic wire to save the universe.
3. "Big man in a suit of armor. Take that off, what are you?"
	• The Avengers: Steve challenges Tony’s core worth outside his tech.
	• Avengers: Age of Ultron / Iron Man 3: Tony proves he is a hero out of the suit, while Spider-Man: Homecoming cements his growth when he tells Peter, "If you're nothing without this suit, then you shouldn't have it."
4. "I don't want to see this on your Myspace page."
	• Iron Man: Tony quips to a young soldier in a humvee right before the Ten Rings attack.
	• Avengers: Endgame: Trapped in space, Tony records a holographic goodbye to Pepper on his helmet: "If you find this recording, don't feel bad about this. Part of the journey is the end."
5. "Peace means having a bigger stick than the other guy."
	• Iron Man: Early Tony explains his warped philosophy on weapons manufacturing.
	• Avengers: Age of Ultron: Tony doubles down on this flaw, attempting to build a "suit of armor around the world" which accidentally births Ultron.
6. "I had to stop him. I knew he was right."
	• Captain America: Civil War: Tony justifies his stance on signing the Sokovia Accords after a mother confronts him over her son's death.
	• Avengers: Infinity War / Endgame: When Tony screams at Steve about his original warning from years prior, it shows the mental toll of knowing the threat was real all along.
7. "That's how it is? A total stranger?"
	• Iron Man 2: Tony acts defensive and snappy when Nick Fury assigns Natasha Romanoff to monitor his erratic behavior.
	• Avengers: Endgame: After Natasha dies on Vormir, Tony sits by the lake, grieving heavily, showing she became the furthest thing from a stranger.
8. "Cheeseburgers."
	• Iron Man: The first thing Tony wants upon escaping captivity in the desert is an American cheeseburger.
	• Avengers: Endgame: At Tony's funeral, Happy Hogan asks young Morgan Stark what she wants to eat, and she replies "cheeseburgers," breaking Happy's heart.
9. "I love you tons."
	• Avengers: Endgame: Tony tells Morgan Stark, "I love you tons," to which she replies, "I love you 3,000."
	• Avengers: Endgame: Tony’s pre-recorded hologram gives the final line of his funeral to his family: "I love you 3,000."
10. "You will be fine."
	• Iron Man: Yinsen tells a dying Tony Stark that his family is dead, but he is going to see them: "It's okay. You will be fine."
	• Avengers: Endgame: Pepper Potts holds a dying Tony's face and delivers the exact same comfort: "Tony, look at me. We're going to be okay. You can rest now."
The Legacy of Steve Rogers (Captain America)
11. "I can do this all day."
	• Captain America: The First Avenger: A pre-serum Steve says this while taking a brutal beating in a Brooklyn alleyway.
	• Captain America: Civil War: Steve says this to Iron Man during their climactic showdown to protect Bucky.
	• Avengers: Endgame: 2012 Steve says it to 2019 Steve, who replies with an exhausted, "Yeah, I know, I know."
12. "I had a date."
	• Captain America: The First Avenger: Steve wakes up in the 21st century, realizes he was frozen for 70 years, and laments his missed dance with Peggy.
	• Avengers: Endgame: Steve returns from the past as an old man, passing the shield to Sam. When asked about his life, he smiles, finally having kept that date.
13. "On your left."
	• Captain America: The Winter Soldier: Steve repeatedly taunts Sam Wilson while lapping him during a morning run around Washington D.C.
	• Avengers: Endgame: As Steve stands alone against Thanos's entire army, Sam’s voice crackles over the radio: "Cap, it's Sam. Can you hear me? On your left."
14. "Before we get started, does anyone want to get out?"
	• Captain America: The Winter Soldier: Steve says this to the Hydra strike team right before the iconic elevator fight.
	• Avengers: Endgame: Steve enters the elevator with the same Hydra agents during the Time Heist. He leans in, mimics the tension, but bypasses the fight entirely by whispering, "Hail Hydra."
15. "He's a friend!"
	• Captain America: Civil War: Steve begs Tony to stop attacking Bucky, justifying his protection with, "He's my friend." Tony replies: "So was I."
	• Thor: Ragnarok: Thor uses a comedic spin on this line when seeing the Hulk in the Grandmaster's arena: "We know each other! He’s a friend from work!"
16. "I'm with you till the end of the line."
	• Captain America: The First Avenger: Bucky tells Steve this to comfort him after his mother's funeral.
	• Captain America: The Winter Soldier: Steve drops his shield and refuses to fight a brainwashed Bucky, repeating the line back to him to break his Hydra conditioning.
17. "That is America's ass."
	• Avengers: Endgame: Tony mocks Steve's old 2012 uniform, but Scott Lang defends it.
	• Avengers: Endgame: After knocking out his 2012 self, Steve stands over his own unconscious body, looks down, and approves: "That is America's ass."
18. "A good man."
	• Captain America: The First Avenger: Dr. Erskine tells Steve before the procedure: "Stay who you are. Not a perfect soldier, but a good man."
	• The Falcon and the Winter Soldier: The weight of this phrase defines Sam Wilson's journey as he wrestles with the shield, ultimately realizing he must carry Cap's legacy as a good man, not a tool of the military.
19. "Avengers... Assemble."
	• Avengers: Age of Ultron: The movie cuts to black right as Steve says the first word, teasing the fans.
	• Avengers: Endgame: Facing down Thanos's army with the unified front of the universe behind him, Steve finally delivers the full, historic phrase.
The Tragedy of Thor & Loki
20. "He's adopted."
	• The Avengers: Thor defends Loki to the Avengers, saying, "He is an Asgardian, and he is my brother!" When Natasha points out Loki killed 80 people, Thor quickly pivots: "He's adopted."
	• Thor: Ragnarok: The Grandmaster asks Loki if he knows Thor. Loki nervously tries to preserve his social standing by blurting out, "I've never seen this man in my life! ... He's adopted."
21. "I went for the head."
	• Avengers: Infinity War: Thanos mocks Thor right before the Snap: "You should have gone for the head."
	• Avengers: Endgame: After decoupling Thanos's head in the garden, a hollow, depressed Thor tells the remaining Avengers, "I went for the head."
22. "Are you Thor, the God of Hammers?"
	• Thor: An arrogant Thor relies entirely on Mjolnir for his identity.
	• Thor: Ragnarok: Odin prompts Thor’s awakening by asking him this question, reminding him that the power was within him all along, not the weapon.
23. "That's how it feels!"
	• The Avengers: Hulk violently smashes Loki back and forth like a ragdoll in Stark Tower.
	• Thor: Ragnarok: Hulk executes the exact same smash on Thor. Sitting in the VIP box, Loki jumps to his feet cheering, "Yes! That's how it feels!"
24. "I'm still worthy."
	• Thor: Thor screams in agony when he fails to lift Mjolnir in the SHIELD compound.
	• Avengers: Endgame: An overweight, depressed Thor travels back to 2013, reaches out his hand, and cries tears of joy when Mjolnir flies into his palm: "I'm still worthy."
25. "The sun will shine on us again."
	• Thor: Ragnarok: Loki says goodbye to Asgard, looking uncertainly toward the stars.
	• Avengers: Infinity War: Facing certain death at the hands of Thanos, Loki looks at his defeated brother and gives him hope one last time: "I assure you, brother, the sun will shine on us again."
Natasha Romanoff & Clint Barton (Black Widow & Hawkeye)
26. "Just like Budapest all over again."
	• The Avengers: Natasha fights off Chitauri forces alongside Clint and compares it to their past mission. Clint replies: "You and I remember Budapest very differently."
	• Avengers: Endgame: Flying the Benatar through space toward Vormir, Natasha smiles and notes they are a long way from Budapest.
27. "Red in my ledger."
	• The Avengers: Natasha confesses to Loki that she is driven by a deep need to wipe out the terrible things she did as a brainwashed KBG assassin.
	• Avengers: Endgame: Natasha refuses to stop tracking targets even after the world collapses, telling Steve, "I used to have nothing. Then I got this family... and I was better because of it."
28. "Let me go."
	• Avengers: Age of Ultron: Natasha drops Bruce Banner down a chute to force him to trigger the Hulk.
	• Avengers: Endgame: Dangling over the cliff on Vormir, Natasha looks at a crying Clint, tells him, "It's okay," and says, "Let me go," sacrificing herself for the Soul Stone.
29. "Don't give me hope."
	• Avengers: Endgame: Clint tells Natasha this when she finds him acting as Ronin in Tokyo, offering a way to bring his family back.
	• Hawkeye (Series): Clint struggles with the same emotional barrier when trying to move past his grief and protect Kate Bishop.
The Growth of Peter Parker (Spider-Man)
30. "If you're nothing without the suit..."
	• Spider-Man: Homecoming: Tony takes Peter's Stark-tech suit away to teach him a lesson about internal responsibility.
	• Spider-Man: No Way Home: Stranded without his advanced technology or mentor, Peter constructs his own classic fabric suit from scratch, finally embodying Tony's lesson.
31. "C'mon, Spider-Man!"
	• Spider-Man: Homecoming: Trapped under tons of rubble by the Vulture, a crying Peter catches his reflection in a puddle and screams this to pull himself up.
	• Spider-Man: No Way Home: Facing his multiversal variants, the line transitions into a realization of what being Spider-Man truly demands—loss and perseverance.
32. "I just wanted to be like you."
	• Spider-Man: Homecoming: A reckless Peter tries to justify his dangerous actions to Tony Stark. Tony responds: "And I wanted you to be better."
	• Spider-Man: Far From Home: Staring at a hologram of Iron Man, Peter realizes he doesn't need to fill Tony's shoes; he needs to be his own hero.
Wanda Maximoff & Vision
33. "I can't feel you."
	• Avengers: Infinity War: Wanda places her hand on Vision’s vibranium forehead, using her magic to feel his consciousness.
	• WandaVision: Wanda visits the SWORD headquarters and stands over Vision's disassembled body. She places her hand on his head, realizes his spark is gone, and whispers, "I can't feel you."
34. "We are an unusual couple."
	• WandaVision (Episode 1): Spoken during their idealized 1950s sitcom lifestyle.
	• WandaVision (Episode 9): Spoken as the Hex collapses, transforming a quirky sit-com joke into a heartbreaking acknowledgment of their tragic reality.
35. "What is grief...?"
	• Avengers: Age of Ultron: Wanda reels from the sudden loss of her twin brother, Pietro.
	• WandaVision: Vision comforts a grieving Wanda with the iconic phrase: "But what is grief, if not love persevering?"
The Cosmic Guardians & Wakanda
36. "We are Groot."
	• Guardians of the Galaxy: Groot expands his branches to shield his new friends from a crashing spaceship, changing his signature phrase for the first time.
	• Avengers: Infinity War: As Groot turns to dust following the Snap, Rocket watches in horror. Director James Gunn later confirmed Groot’s final, weak "I am Groot" translated to: "Dad?"
37. "He may have been your father, boy, but he wasn't your daddy."
	• Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2: Yondu tells Peter Quill this right before sacrificing his own spacesuit to save Peter in the vacuum of space.
	• Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3: Peter Quill finally returns to Earth to reconnect with his actual, human grandfather, honoring the family lessons Yondu died to teach him.
38. "Wakanda Forever!"
	• Black Panther: T'Challa uses the salute as a rallying battle cry for his nation.
	• Black Panther: Wakanda Forever: Queen Ramonda and Shuri use the phrase not as a war cry, but as a solemn tribute to the memory of T'Challa.
39. "Vengeance has consumed you."
	• Captain America: Civil War: T'Challa refuses to kill Zemo, stating, "Vengeance has consumed you. It's consuming them. I'm done letting it consume me."
	• Black Panther: T'Challa tries to offer mercy to Killmonger, trying to break the cycle of historic pain that consumed his cousin.
Multi-Character Parallels & Cosmic Rhymes
40. "Fine. I'll do it myself."
	• Avengers: Age of Ultron: Thanos grabs the Infinity Gauntlet out of his vault, tired of his subordinates failing him.
	• Avengers: Endgame: Mid-battle, Thanos abandons his complex grand plans and resorts to brutal, physical warfare to take the stones by force.
41. "I recognize the council has made a decision..."
	• The Avengers: Nick Fury rejects the World Security Council's order to nuke Manhattan.
	• Captain America: The Winter Soldier: Fury uses his dead eye to override a corrupted SHIELD system, showing he always answers to a higher moral code.
42. "I had strings, but now I'm free."
	• Avengers: Age of Ultron: Ultron sings the Pinocchio song to mock the Avengers' ties to authority.
	• WandaVision / Multiverse of Madness: Wanda adopts this dark philosophy as the Scarlet Witch, tearing through realities because she refuses to be bound by the rules of others.
43. "Dormammu, I've come to bargain."
	• Doctor Strange: Strange traps a cosmic entity in an infinite time loop, dying thousands of times to save Earth.
	• Spider-Man: No Way Home: Strange finds himself on the receiving end of a stubborn hero (Peter) altering reality, flipping his position from the controller of chaos to the one trying to contain it.
44. "Boom! You looking for this?"
	• Avengers: Age of Ultron: War Machine (Rhodey) tells a boring military story that falls flat with Thor and Tony.
	• Avengers: Age of Ultron: Rhodey says it later to regular civilians after saving them, finally getting the validation he wanted.
45. "Magic's just science we don't understand yet."
	• Thor: Thor explains Asgardian technology to Jane Foster in the desert.
	• Thor: Love and Thunder: Jane Foster repeats this phrase back as Mighty Thor, having bridged the gap between human science and cosmic divinity.
46. "Get help!"
	• Thor: Ragnarok: Thor forces a reluctant Loki to play a humiliating childhood distraction game to get past Sakaarian guards.
	• Avengers: Infinity War: Thor laments the loss of Loki, missing the brother who used to play "Get Help" with him.
47. "Is this your king?"
	• Black Panther: Killmonger screams this to the citizens of Wakanda while beating T'Challa at Warrior Falls.
	• Black Panther: Wakanda Forever: The phrase echoes visually when Namor brings Wakanda to its knees, forcing Shuri to step up as the new protector.
48. "You just don't know when to give up."
	• Captain America: The First Avenger: A bully tells this to a bloody Steve Rogers in the alley.
	• Avengers: Endgame: Thanos states this implicitly to the remaining Avengers as they refuse to stop fighting him in the ruins of their headquarters.
49. "Tell me something I don't know."
	• Iron Man: Tony tells Jarvis this while testing flight dynamics.
	• Black Widow: Natasha and Yelena use quick, cynical dialogue exchanges to mask their underlying trauma and bond as sisters.
50. "Part of the journey is the end."
	• Avengers: Endgame: Tony Stark starts the movie with this line recorded in space.
	• Avengers: Endgame: The entire Infinity Saga concludes with this exact thematic philosophy, closing out a 22-movie overarching dialogue parallel.
