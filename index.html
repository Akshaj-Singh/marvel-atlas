ABOUT THE ATLAS

Connections, not just credits.

Explore the on-screen Marvel multiverse, its characters, creators, crossovers, callbacks and artifacts in one interactive map. The site is a static learning project.

Evidence matters. Marvel links labeled “Marvel official” cite an official source. Legacy graph connections and the 1,380 supplied observations are visibly marked as editorial or user-supplied, not independently fact-checked. A collection link only means two nodes belong to the same browsing category: it does not mean they share canon.

A research link is shown only when a relationship has an attached URL, with its scope and publisher identified. The presence of an external link does not turn a fan-wiki or editorial interpretation into confirmed canon. All 50 supplied callback comparisons are indexed as navigable nodes. Some are thematic or within one film, not verified repeated dialogue; those distinctions are stated. The new story-beat connections are editorial annotations unless independently sourced.

Upcoming entries are based on the dated source notes. A missing date is shown as unknown rather than guessed.
